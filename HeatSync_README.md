# ❄️ HeatSync — Thermal-Aware Cooling Advisor for Mission-Critical Data Centers

> **FortyGuard Hackathon 2026** | Track: **Future Buildings & Energy** / **Industrial & Enterprise**

HeatSync is an intelligent thermal copilot that bridges FortyGuard's street-level, human-relevant microclimate AI with data center cooling automation. It translates hourly atmospheric feeds — including apparent temperature, wet-bulb depression, air quality (PM2.5, NO₂, CO₂), and solar irradiance — into optimal cooling dispatch decisions (Free-Air Economization, Direct Evaporative, or Mechanical DX Chilling), quantifying PUE improvements, utility cost savings, and avoided grid emissions in real time.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem

- **The Problem:** Cooling accounts for up to 40% of total electrical power consumed by data center infrastructure. Most facilities operate on rigid, static schedules with constant mechanical DX chilling, ignoring ambient microclimate windows that permit energy-efficient economization.
- **The Solution:** HeatSync ingests FortyGuard's environmental telemetry (`POST /v1/env_params` and `POST /v1/heatmap`) to dynamically match cooling modes to psychrometric thresholds and safety criteria (ASHRAE standards).
- **The Measured Impact:** Across evaluated facilities (Ashburn VA, Phoenix AZ, and San José CA), HeatSync identifies up to 10–24 daily eco-cooling hours, driving average facility PUE from a baseline of 1.55 down to 1.10–1.25, generating over $3,800/day in utility savings for a standard 10 MW facility and preventing over 17 metric tons of CO₂e emissions daily per location.

## 3. Deep-Dive Technical Modules & Mathematical Models

### 3.1. FortyGuard Environmental Ingestion Schema (`data_pipeline.py`)

The ingestion layer parses structured JSON objects generated by FortyGuard's `POST /v1/env_params` endpoint, standardizing 24-hour temporal data into normalized vectors:

- **Thermal Metrics:** Apparent Air Temperature (T_apparent, °C), Wet-Bulb Temperature (T_wb, °C), Heat Index (T_hi, °C), Relative Humidity (RH, %)
- **Atmospheric & Solar:** Cloud Cover (octas), Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI, W/m²), Direct Normal Irradiance (DNI, W/m²), Diffuse Horizontal Irradiance (DHI, W/m²)
- **Air Quality & Particulate Telemetry:** PM2.5 Index, PM10 Index, NO₂ Index, SO₂ Index, O₃ Index, CO Index, Methane (ppb), and CO₂ (ppm)

### 3.2. ASHRAE Psychrometric Decision Engine (`cooling_engine.py`)

Data center HVAC optimization is bounded by the psychrometric properties of moist air and particulate intake restrictions to prevent server component degradation, fan failure, and electrostatic discharge.

**Decision Logic Matrix:**

1. **Free-Air Direct Economizer:**
   Triggered when: T_apparent ≤ 19.0°C **AND** RH < 85.0% **AND** PM2.5 < 55.0
   - *Mechanism:* Outside air is filtered and introduced directly into the server intake plenum with zero mechanical compressor lift.

2. **Direct / Indirect Evaporative (Adiabatic Cooling):**
   Triggered when: T_wb ≤ 18.5°C **AND** T_apparent ≤ 27.0°C **AND** PM2.5 < 60.0
   - *Mechanism:* Exploits wet-bulb depression (ΔT = T_apparent − T_wb) by evaporating water into the intake air stream, reducing dry-bulb temperature along the constant wet-bulb line with minimal pumping/fan power.

3. **Mechanical Direct Expansion (DX) Chiller:**
   Triggered when: T_apparent > 27.0°C **OR** T_wb > 18.5°C **OR** PM2.5 ≥ 55.0 (during economizer mode)
   - *Mechanism:* Sealed-loop vapor compression refrigeration cycles run at full operational lift, consuming peak electrical power.

### 3.3. Energy, PUE & Carbon Mathematical Formulations (`efficiency_model.py`)

Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) is defined as:

```
PUE = P_Total Facility / P_IT Load
```

Where `P_Total Facility = P_IT Load + P_Cooling + P_Power Distribution Loss`.

**Operating Mode PUE Constants:**

| Mode | PUE |
|---|---|
| Baseline (Mechanical DX) | 1.55 |
| Evaporative | 1.25 |
| Free-Air Economizer | 1.10 |

**Hourly Savings and Emission Formulations**

For an IT load capacity `L_IT` (kW) and active mode `PUE_t` at hour `t`:

1. **Power Consumption:**
   - `P_baseline,t = L_IT × PUE_Baseline`
   - `P_optimized,t = L_IT × PUE_t`

2. **Hourly Electrical Energy Saved (kWh):**
   `E_saved,t = (P_baseline,t − P_optimized,t) × 1 hour`

3. **Utility Cost Savings ($):**
   `S_cost,t = E_saved,t × R_electricity`
   where `R_electricity` is the local utility rate ($/kWh).

4. **Avoided Carbon Emissions (kg CO₂e):**
   `C_avoided,t = E_saved,t × I_grid`
   where `I_grid = 0.385 kg CO₂/kWh` (U.S. Environmental Protection Agency grid average).

### 3.4. LangGraph State Machine Architecture (`orchestration_graph.py`)

The pipeline relies on `langgraph.graph.StateGraph` utilizing a strongly-typed `HeatSyncState`:

```python
class HeatSyncState(TypedDict):
    facility_name: str
    selected_hour: int
    facility_meta: Dict[str, Any]
    raw_df: Optional[pd.DataFrame]
    processed_df: Optional[pd.DataFrame]
    current_metrics: Dict[str, Any]
    kpis: Dict[str, Any]
    alerts: List[Dict[str, Any]]
    dispatch_recommendation: Optional[Dict[str, Any]]
    narrative: str
    errors: List[str]
```

**Graph Node Responsibilities:**

1. `node_ingest_data` — Reads structured JSON telemetry from `data/{facility_name}_env.json`.
2. `node_run_decision_engine` — Evaluates vector conditions against ASHRAE thresholds.
3. `node_compute_efficiency` — Calculates delta PUE, $, and kg CO₂e across the 24-hour horizon.
4. `node_scan_alerts` — Evaluates the upcoming 12-hour window (T+1h to T+12h) for thermal risks (T_apparent ≥ 28°C) and particulate spikes (PM2.5 ≥ 58).
5. `node_compute_workload_dispatch` — Checks regional peer facilities when the local site requires DX chillers; flags candidate sites for compute workload migration.
6. `node_synthesize_narrative` — Calls `gpt-4o` via LangChain `ChatPromptTemplate` with automated deterministic fallback on network/credential exception.

### 3.5. Dynamic Workload Dispatch Optimization

When a monitored data center enters peak thermal stress requiring active mechanical chiller power, HeatSync inspects alternative geographically distributed facilities within the fleet.

If an alternate site (e.g., San José, CA) is currently in Free-Air Economizer mode (T_apparent ≤ 19°C), the workload router outputs an immediate operational dispatch recommendation to shift non-urgent batch AI training or large ETL pipelines to the cooler node, shaving local peak power demand and avoiding utility coincident-peak demand penalties.
